# The Council records

A Board of four AI agents (the Visionary, the Game Designer, the Realist, the Pitchman) generated, attacked and refined hackathon
game ideas built on the MaleCNS v1.0 fly connectome. These files are the record of that process, kept as it was delivered on
Sat 26 Sept 2026. **The topic docs in `docs/` are the living plan and win if anything here disagrees with them.**

1. [01_phase0_idea_factory.md](01_phase0_idea_factory.md): the fact check, 16 raw concepts, the uniqueness filters, the kills and fights, and the three finalists.
2. [02_party_coop_council.md](02_party_coop_council.md): the team picked the party co-op ("Neck", then "Fly by Committee") and asked for Jackbox-style joining. Steps 1 to 5 for it, plus the first data check.
3. [03_royalty_reskin.md](03_royalty_reskin.md): the event theme turned out to be royalty. The reskin into *His Royal Flyness*, the research, and which tracks to enter.

## The original brief (summary)

- Act as a Board of four agents that generate, attack and refine hackathon game ideas until they're unique and winnable.
- Event: hackUMBC 2026, 24 hours, 4-person team (one first-year UMD bioengineering student with wet lab, ESM-2, GNN and protein-function experience; three solid programmers; no professional artist).
- Format: downloadable desktop game (Godot 4 or Unity suggested) with a Python sidecar running the brain simulation.
- Core tech: MaleCNS v1.0, about 166,000 neurons, structural only. Weights are assumptions and the game must say so.
- Hard filters: no "fly plays an existing game" (Doom, Minecraft, Beat Saber, crypto); no pet/Tamagotchi, trivia or visualizer + chatbot.
  **Ablation test** (must behave differently on a random graph, shown as an in-game toggle). **Why-now test** (needs the male CNS).
  **Player verb** isn't "watch". **30-second test**. No two finalists share a genre or verb.
- Agentic requirement: real agents that call the brain simulation as a tool, with visible checking or self-correction.
- Workflow: Phase 0 (16 concepts, filter, promote 3), Steps 1 to 5 per idea (Visionary, Game Designer, Realist, Pitchman, Consensus),
  then rank and plan the first 2 hours.
- Tone: people actually talking; clear, direct, specific; no buzzwords; no em dashes.
