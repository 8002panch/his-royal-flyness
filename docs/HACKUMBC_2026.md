# hackUMBC 2026: event facts

Summarized from the hackUMBC 2026 Devpost page (read on Sat 26 Sept, about 13:00, "about 23 hours to deadline").
Recheck anything important on Devpost or with an organizer.

## Basics
- **Where:** RAC (Retriever Activities Center), UMBC. In person.
- **When:** Sat 26 to Sun 27 Sept 2026, 24 hours.
- **Who:** college students 18 and over (or enrolled UMBC students under 18). Teams of at most 4, all checked in.
- **Tags:** Beginner Friendly, Education, Open Ended. 108 participants registered on Devpost at the time of reading.
- **Theme:** royalty (announced at the opening ceremony). The Devpost copy uses quest language ("castle, feast, glory").
- **Website:** hackumbc.tech

## Deadlines
| What | When |
|---|---|
| Devpost submission **created** | Sun 27 Sept, 11:00 AM EDT |
| Devpost submission **finalized** (Devpost's own deadline) | Sun 27 Sept, 11:45 AM EDT |

## Submission requirements
- A GitHub link to all code written for the hack, repository set to **public** before attaching.
- A short **demo video, at least 30 seconds**. A screen recording with voiceover is fine.
- One project per team.
- Tracks: enter as many as you like, but each must clearly fit; organizers can remove a project from a track that doesn't.
- Best First Time Hack: every team member must be at their first hackathon.
- **In-person demo of 3 to 5 minutes.** Projects not present to demo aren't judged.
- The 2025 rules also required every commit in the project repo to be inside the hacking window. We assume the same, which is
  why the planning repo is never submitted.

## Judging criteria
1. **Creativity:** how unique, original and innovative.
2. **Technical complexity:** how challenging and well implemented.
3. **Impact:** how well it solves a real problem or addresses an important need.
4. **Execution:** how well it works and delivers on its purpose.

Judges are secret. Game Jamathon entries present to industry professional judges during the judging period.
